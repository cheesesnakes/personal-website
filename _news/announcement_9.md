New publication in *Individual-based Ecology*!

My new paper explores how lethal and non-lethal human interactions can reshape predator–prey dynamics using an agent-based model. In the model, superpredators targeting prey alone sharply reduced mesopredator–prey coexistence, while non-lethal disturbance had small effects at the avoidance costs tested. These are qualitative results from an uncalibrated model, not predictions for any particular ecosystem.
